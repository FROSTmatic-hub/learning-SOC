- The [[Alert Workbook]] might tell the [Alert Assignee](/1.%20Concepts/SOC%20L1%20Triage/alert_assignee.md) on how to deal with the alert
# For example:
- `Suspicious PowerShell Alert` 
- The workbook might tell you to:
	- Check the user.
	- Examine the command.
	- Look at the parent process.
	- Check related network connections.
	- Search for additional activity.
