- MTTR stands for **Mean Time To Respond**
- It tells us: ***how long did it take the [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md) analyst to respond to the threat?***
# For Example
- An attacker attacks at `9:00AM`
- [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md) analyst detects it at `9:10 AM`
- [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md) analyst acknowledges the alert and starts investigating it at `9:15 AM`
- [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md)analyst responds to the threat at `9:20 AM` and isolate the compromised device.
- So, MTTR is **10 mins**

> A respond could be:
	> Isolating a compromised device
	> Raising an Incident
	> Blocking malicious files
	> etc.
