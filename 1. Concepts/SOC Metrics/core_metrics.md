# What are Core Metrics?
- The SOC team performs its purpose by developing, receiving, and triaging alerts. The [SOC Level 1 Analyst](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC_lvl1.md) in this process is to reliably report True Positives to the higher level, to [SOC Level 2 Analyst](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC_lvl2.md).
- The four metrics are:
	1. [Alert Count](/1.%20Concepts/SOC%20Metrics/alert_count.md)
	2. [False Positive Rate](/1.%20Concepts/SOC%20Metrics/FPR.md)
	3. [Alert Escalation Rate](/1.%20Concepts/SOC%20Metrics/AER.md)
	4. [Alert Detection Rate](/1.%20Concepts/SOC%20Metrics/alert_detection.md)
