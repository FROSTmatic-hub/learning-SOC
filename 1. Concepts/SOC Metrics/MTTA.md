- MTTA stands for **Mean Time To Acknowledge**
- It tells us: ***once the alert is generated, how long did it take the [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md) analyst to acknowledge it and start investigating it?***
# For Example
- An attacker attacks at `9:00AM`
- [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md) analyst detects it at `9:10 AM`
- [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md) analyst acknowledges the alert and starts investigating it at `9:15 AM`
- So, MTTA is **5 mins**
