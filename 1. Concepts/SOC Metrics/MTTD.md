- MTTD stands for **Mean Time To Detect**
- It tells us: ***how long did it take the analyst to detect the threat?***
# For example:
- An attacker begins attacking at `9:00 AM`
- [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md) analyst detects the threat at `9:10 AM`
- So, MTTD is **10 mins**
