- After detecting a suspicious malware/threat, [EDR](/1.%20Concepts/Introduction%20to%20EDR/EDR.md) allows the analyst to respond to the threat directly from the [EDR](/1.%20Concepts/Introduction%20to%20EDR/EDR.md) console.
- A [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md) analyst may:
	- **Isolate the [Endpoint](/1.%20Concepts/Introduction%20to%20EDR/endpoint.md) device**
	- **Terminate the Process**
	- **Quarantine the file**
	- **Give remote access to higher [[SOC]]**
	- etc.
