# What is EDR?
- EDR stands for **Endpoint Detection and Response**
- It is a Security Solution that continuously monitors [Endpoint](/1.%20Concepts/Introduction%20to%20EDR/endpoint.md) devices for suspicious acitivies.
- It watches [Endpoint](/1.%20Concepts/Introduction%20to%20EDR/endpoint.md) devices and can alert the [SOC](/1.%20Concepts/Blue%20Team%20Introduction/SOC.md) analyst for malicous threats and investigation.
