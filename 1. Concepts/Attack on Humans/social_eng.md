# What is Social Engineering?
- Social Engineering means manipulating the person into doing something **they are not supposed to do.
- Instead of targeting system vulnerabilities, the attacker focuses on manipulating human psychology. 

# How does it work?
- For Social Engineering to work, the attacker tries to be:
	- **Trustworthy:** the attacker makes the person/program seems legitimate 
	- **Emotional:** the attack creates different emotional feeling such as:
		- Urgency
		- Fear
		- Curiosity
