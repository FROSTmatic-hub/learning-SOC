# What is Phishing Attack?
- Phishing attack is the most common type of [Social Engineering](/1.%20Concepts/Attack%20on%20Humans/social_eng.md)
- For example,
	- The victim receives an email saying: "Your account is not secure! Click on the link to secure it"
	- The victim clicks on the link
	- Fake login page opens
	- The victim enters their credentials
	- The attacker steals their crendentials

> [!NOTE] 
>  Phishing attacks are not limited to emails. The same idea can be applied on apps, websites, social media, etc.
